Section I

Third-party SDK traffic on the watchlist

In 60 s on the idle watchlist the app contacted 9 third-party domains (Plotline, VWO, Google Firebase, CleverTap, MoEngage, Protectt.ai, AppsFlyer). Most of these SDKs are started for the whole app, not by this module, so the main item is a Product and BE decision. The watchlist itself mounts a Plotline banner and a Plotline-tracked kebab on every open, which is worth checking.

Ask Product and BE which of the SDKs seen on the idle watchlist (Plotline, VWO, Firebase, CleverTap, MoEngage, Protectt.ai, AppsFlyer) must talk to the network there, and whether batching/flush intervals can be raised.

Teams ProductBERisk LowEffort S · ≤ 1 dayNo visible change

Today

X3 counted 9 domains first seen within the 60 s capture while the app was idle on Bench 20. The SDKs are initialised at app level (apps/super_app); this plan did not analyse their initialisation line by line.

What to change

  1. Collect each SDK's network behaviour with PCAPdroid per SDK domain.
  2. Product decides; BE/marketing change dashboard settings where possible.

I2

Check what the Plotline widgets do on each open

The watchlist mounts PlotlineWidget(watchlistBannerKey) above the list on every open and wraps the kebab in a Plotline PView. Check with the Plotline SDK whether these widgets fetch or report on mount when no campaign targets them, and render them only when a campaign is active if they do.

Teams FEProductRisk LowEffort S · ≤ 1 dayNo visible changeverify on device

Today

When the header part of the body builds for WatchlistLoaded, it inserts const PlotlineWidget(valueKey: PlotLineWidgetKey.watchlistBannerKey). The kebab menu is wrapped in PView(valueKey: PlotlineCampaignEvent.kebabMenuInWatchlistEvent). Plotline is one of the 9 third-party domains seen in X3. Whether these widgets cause network calls is not visible from this repo (SDK code) (verify with PCAPdroid filtered to Plotline's domain while opening the tab).

What to change

  1. Open/close the tab 10 times with PCAPdroid filtered to Plotline; count requests.
  2. If requests scale with opens, ask Plotline for a campaign-presence check and gate the widget.
