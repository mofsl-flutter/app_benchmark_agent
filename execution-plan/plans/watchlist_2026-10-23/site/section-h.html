Section H

Watchlist features and quick actions

The watchlist shows live price and change, holdings quantity and unrealised P&L, and offers Buy/Sell by swiping a row. Market depth, alerts and an order ticket open only on the full quote page, and rows have no mini chart. Both items are Product decisions first; the FE work reuses widgets that already exist in the quote module and stays behind a flag.

H1

Quick-action sheet over the watchlist

Add a bottom sheet over the list with Buy, Sell, 5-level market depth and Create Alert for a stock, opened from a row without leaving the watchlist. Long-press opens edit mode today, so the trigger is a Product choice. Behind a flag.

Teams FEProductRisk HighEffort L · 1 week +Visible to usersDepends on A1

Today

The listing's InkWell long-press checks connectivity, logs a CleverTap event and calls switchToEditMode; a tap logs analytics and pushes the quote page with MoGetQuoteNavigator.navigateToScrip. Swiping a row calls searchToPlaceOrderNavigation with BUY or SELL (watchlist_listing.dart 420-456). Depth and alerts exist only on the quote page.

What to change

  1. Product picks the gesture and actions.
  2. Build the sheet as a StreamingBaseWidget so depth subscription stops when it closes (stopBidStreaming).
  3. Reuse searchToPlaceOrderNavigation for Buy/Sell and the quote module's alert entry point.
  4. Log the same analytics events the quote page logs for these actions, with source 'watchlist_sheet'.

H2

Intraday sparkline on watchlist rows

Rows have no mini chart. A sparkline needs an intraday price series per symbol from BE (or a client-built series from ticks since open) and a Product decision on space in the row.

Teams FEBEProductRisk HighEffort L · 1 week +Visible to usersDepends on A1

Today

rightColumnLtp shows LTP, an arrow SVG and change/% only. There is no price history on the watchlist.

What to change

  1. Agree API with BE.
  2. One CustomPainter per row with a fixed size and RepaintBoundary; repaint only when the series changes (not on every tick).
  3. Fetch series once per open and every 5 min; append the last tick to the final point only if Product wants it live.
