A · Idle CPU and redraw on the watchlist

With nobody touching the phone, the Bench 20 watchlist keeps about two CPU cores busy and redraws about 40 times a second, and half of those frames are identical to the one before. The code shows three sources: every price packet rebuilds a whole row even when the shown numbers did not change, the header wealth tile rebuilds on every holdings tick and animates every 3 s, and new tick streams are created on every build. These changes keep every price, colour and animation the user sees, but stop the work that draws the same pixels again.

Each price packet rebuilds the whole row, even when the packet only changed a field the row does not show. Pass a packet to the row only when the text it displays would change, and keep the static left column out of the tick builder.

Teams FERisk MediumEffort M · 2–4 daysNo visible changeverify on deviceDepends on A1 A3

Today

The broadcaster keeps one BroadcastResponse2 per symbol, mutates it in place and re-publishes it for every packet (mosl_broadcaster broadcast_channel.dart 243-251), so every packet for a symbol rebuilds the whole row, including packets that change only volume, OI or depth when the row shows LTP and change.
