Section D

Live prices and returning to the app

On the watchlist, RELIANCE's price changed 17 times a minute, always on whole seconds, and after a minute in the background the app came back showing old prices for about a second before they moved. The client code passes every packet straight to the rows (no throttle), so the 1 s cadence most likely comes from the feed and needs a BE answer. The stale prices on return come from the socket being closed 15 s after the app is paused, so it must reconnect and log in again. One change is a BE question, the other a configurable socket delay; the visual 'updating' marker is E2.

Change details

D1

Ask BE whether the quote feed is conflated to 1 s

The client publishes every quote packet as it arrives and the watchlist renders each one, yet RELIANCE changed only on whole seconds. Ask BE whether the regular (non tick-by-tick) quote feed sends at most one update per symbol per second, and whether a faster feed exists for the watchlist.

Teams BERisk LowEffort S · ≤ 1 dayNo visible changeverify on device

Today

PacketPublisher.publish adds every parsed quote to a PublishSubject; BroadcastChannelImpl.onBroadcast updates the cached response and publishes it immediately. No throttle, debounce, sample or buffer exists in mosl_broadcaster 3deab191 (searched). The watchlist rows render each packet (watchlist_item.dart StreamBuilder). The one-run U1 reading shows changes on whole seconds, which points to the feed (verify with a packet timestamp log on a debug build).

What to change

  1. Debug build: log arrival time and LTP of RELIANCE packets for 2 min in market hours; check spacing.
  2. Send the BE question with the log.
  3. If BE offers a faster feed, plan it as a separate change with the data cost.

D2

Keep the price socket open longer in background

The broadcaster closes the price socket 15 s after the app is paused, so a user who comes back after a minute waits for a reconnect and login while old prices are shown. Make the delay configurable from Remote Config, default 15 s, and test 90–120 s.

Teams FE · sharedBEProductRisk MediumEffort S · ≤ 1 dayVisible to usersverify on device

Today

BroadCastHelper._init builds BroadcastOptions without shutdownDelay, so the default of 15 s applies (mosl_socket_core broadcast_option.dart line 45). On pause, BroadcastController starts that timer and then stops streaming (broadcast_controller.dart 152-158, 162-169). After 60 s away the socket is closed; on return it must connect, log in, and replay subscriptions (broadcast_channel.dart 213-227) before the first tick. Rows meanwhile show the cached BroadcastResponse2 values.

What to change

  1. In mosl_broadcaster, add static Duration shutdownDelay = BroadcastOptions.defaultShutdownDelay; to BroadCastHelper and pass it in _init (additive; default unchanged).
  2. In the app, set it before the first BroadCastHelper.instance access. If Remote Config is not active yet at that point, use the value stored at the previous launch.
  3. Test 15 s (today), 90 s and 120 s: U3, background data (C4-style), battery over 1 h.

How existing behaviour is kept

  • Default value equals today's 15 s.
  • Rollback is a Remote Config change (applies from next launch).
  • Network loss still stops streaming immediately (unchanged branch in _handleAppEvent2).

Regression checks

  • Background 10 s, 60 s, 3 min and return: prices resume; no duplicate subscriptions.
  • Logout while in background, login as another user: socket re-authenticates.
  • Airplane mode in background: no crash on return.

Risk Medium. Shared socket for the whole app. Default stays 15 s. A longer value keeps a socket and its traffic alive in background, which affects battery, data and server connections.

Product decision needed

May the live-price connection stay open for up to 2 minutes after the user leaves the app (so prices are live immediately on return), at the cost of some background data? Today it closes after 15 s.

Backend request

Remote Config integer broadcast_shutdown_delay_seconds (default 15). Confirm the broadcast servers can hold the extra idle connections.

After improvement

With a 90–120 s delay, a 60 s absence no longer needs a reconnect, so the first tick after return should arrive with the next market update (U3 towards ≈ 0.5 s, to be confirmed).
