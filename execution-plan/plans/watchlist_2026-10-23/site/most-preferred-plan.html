Most Preferred Plan

For each factor that makes the screen feel slow, this plan names the causes that contribute the most and picks only the changes that remove them. Smaller wins from the full plan are left for later.

46 changes29 low · 13 medium · 4 high risk25 files

Problem statement

The RISE watchlist already does the hard parts: live prices on every row, holdings quantity and unrealised P&L on the row, swipe to trade, smooth tab switching and no crashes. But it does far more work than it shows, keeps memory it no longer needs and gives no sign when its prices are old. Measured on an OPPO CPH3009 (Android 17, 120 Hz) with a 20-stock list:

  • Sitting idle on the list uses 185.5 % CPU (almost two cores) and redraws 39.6 times a second; 49.5 % of those frames are identical to the one before.
  • Scrolling delivers 75.3 fps on a 120 Hz screen (about 63 % of the frames offered) even though janky frames are only 0.34 %.
  • Opening and closing the watchlist ten times adds 60 MB that is not released; after five minutes the app holds 380.5 MB.
  • Offline, prices stay on screen but look live: no banner, no last-updated time (offline score 44.4). After a minute in the background, old prices show before live ones resume.
  • Release builds write the client code, cached responses and false 'API Failed' lines to the device log.

These come from a small number of causes in the code: live ticks rebuild whole rows and the header even when nothing visible changed, the header rebuilds on every scroll frame, rows are recreated on every list update and leave listeners behind, and the offline signals are either gated off or missing. The front-end changes below remove those causes; items that need BE or a Product decision are listed as left for later.
